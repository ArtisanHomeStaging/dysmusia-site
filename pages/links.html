Links & Organizations

Where to go for evaluation, advocacy, teacher training, and further reading.

Dyslexia advocacy · US

International Dyslexia Association

A long-established nonprofit devoted to dyslexia and related language-based learning differences, offering fact sheets, referrals, and a peer-reviewed journal. A useful starting point even though its focus is language rather than music specifically.

Dyslexia advocacy · UK

British Dyslexia Association

Publishes, with Music Teacher Magazine, a free "Teacher Guide to Music and Dyslexia" — one of the most directly relevant resources available for music educators specifically.

Research lab

BRAMS (Montreal)

A research center for music cognition where much of the foundational work on congenital amusia and pitch perception, including Ayotte and Peretz's research, has been based.

General learning-differences resource

Understood.org

A nonprofit resource covering dyslexia, ADHD, and other learning differences broadly, including a plain-language explainer specifically on dyslexia and music.

Advocacy · US

Learning Disabilities Association of America

A national organization offering general information and resources for learning disabilities, useful alongside more music-specific sources.

Teaching credential

Academy of Orton-Gillingham Practitioners and Educators

The reference body for Orton-Gillingham, the multisensory literacy approach that much of multisensory music teaching borrows its logic from.

Dysmusia

An independent, informational project exploring why some capable musicians struggle to read music — and finding better ways to teach them. Not a diagnostic tool or medical advice.
